Video analysis

Video analysis for basketball referees

Watching your game back is the best way to improve. You just need to find the right plays in 90 minutes of footage. REF CLIPS, the video analysis app from the Referee Tracker family, turns your game into clips ready to review or send to your referee coach.

Why review your games on video

  • Check your calls with a cool head: what you called, and what you should have called.
  • Work on your positioning and your rotations with your partner.
  • Prepare a talk with your referee coach or assessor, with clips to back it up.
  • Keep a record of your progress from one game to the next.

How REF CLIPS works

  1. You open your game video, from your computer or from a link sent by the club (Drive, Dropbox, WeTransfer).
  2. You type the call code on the keyboard while it plays: "travelling", "unsportsmanlike"… Press Enter and the moment is tagged with the right quarter.
  3. You draw on the picture: an arrow, a circle round your position, and the video holds on your drawing for a few seconds.
  4. REF CLIPS cuts each play into a clip, up to 4K, and creates a clear PDF report of your game.

Frequently asked questions

How do I review a refereed game on video?

Watch your game and tag each call as it happens, then go back over the plays one by one. REF CLIPS automates this: a keyboard code tags the play, the app cuts the clip and prepares a PDF report.

What video analysis software is there for basketball referees?

REF CLIPS is a video analysis app built for basketball referees, on Mac and Windows: keyboard tagging, precise clips, drawing on the picture and a PDF report.

Can I use the video a club sends me?

Yes: paste the Drive, Dropbox or WeTransfer link and REF CLIPS fetches the video.

Do I need a separate account for REF CLIPS?

No. REF CLIPS opens with your Referee Tracker account, and your games show up straight away.
