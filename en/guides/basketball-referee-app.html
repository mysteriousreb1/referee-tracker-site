App

The basketball referee app and tracking software

A basketball referee handles a lot more than games: appointments arriving by e-mail, travel, match fees paid by several payers, reports, training. Referee Tracker brings it all together in one app, on your phone and your computer.

Why a referee needs a tracking app

Over a season, a basketball referee often gets dozens of appointments, plus their changes and cancellations. Each one means a trip, a match fee, a payer and a payment date. Keeping all that in a notebook or a spreadsheet takes time, and slip-ups are costly: a game you forgot to log, a payment that never came, a meeting time you missed.

Referee tracking software does that work for you: it logs every game, works out what you are owed and reminds you at the right time.

What Referee Tracker does

What the referee needsWhat the app does
Log your gamesYou forward your appointments: each game is created on its own, then updated if it is changed or cancelled.
Work out your match feesFixed fee plus round-trip mileage from your address, using your rate scale, to the cent.

Review your games on videoWith REF CLIPS, the video analysis app from the Referee Tracker family.

How it works, in three steps

  1. You set up a forwarding rule in your mailbox (Gmail, Outlook, Orange, iCloud…) to your personal address firstname@referee-tracker.fr. Two minutes, once.
  2. The app reads each appointment: date, meeting time, venue, teams, level, your role, your partner.
  3. You track and get paid: mileage, match fee, expected payment date, a reminder the day before and an alert if a payment is late.
Referee Tracker never accesses your mailbox: only the e-mails you forward reach the app.

5x5 and 3x3, every level

The app handles 5x5 basketball appointments as well as 3x3 tournaments, from county level up to national championships. For 3x3, just forward the tournament appointment e-mail: the app finds the venue, your time slot and your match fee.

On your phone and your computer

Referee Tracker runs in the browser, with nothing to install: on your phone, you add it to your home screen like an app, with its notifications; on a computer, you get the big screen for your season analysis, yearly export and reports.

Your data stays yours

  • Data hosted in the European Union, encrypted connections.
  • A separate space for each referee, no bank details asked for.
  • Export or deletion on simple request.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best app for a basketball referee?

Referee Tracker is an app built for basketball referees: it reads your appointments, creates your games, works out mileage and match fees, tracks payments and helps you improve (rules, quizzes, video analysis with REF CLIPS).

Is there tracking software for basketball referees?

Yes. Referee Tracker runs on your phone and your computer, in the browser, with nothing to install. It tracks your games, match fees and payments for the whole season.

Do I have to enter my games by hand?

No. You forward your appointments by e-mail (or set up an automatic forwarding rule): each game is created and updated on its own.

Does the app handle 3x3?

Yes. Forward the 3x3 tournament appointment e-mail: the app finds the venue, your time slot and your match fee, and works out your mileage.

Is Referee Tracker an official tool?

No. It is an independent project by a referee, not affiliated with any basketball body. The official appointment always prevails.
