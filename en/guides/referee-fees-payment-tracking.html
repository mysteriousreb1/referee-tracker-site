Payments

Tracking your basketball referee match fees and payments

County committee, regional league, federation, clubs: a basketball referee gets paid by several bodies, each on its own schedule. The result: it is hard to know what you have already been paid and what you are still owed. Here is a simple method, and the tool that applies it for you.

The problem: several payers, several schedules

  • Different payers depending on the level of the game: county committee, regional league, federation, or the home club directly.
  • Different due dates: paid on the night of the game, at the end of the month, every quarter…
  • Grouped transfers with no breakdown: one lump sum for several games, and it is up to you to guess which.
  • Rounding on mileage that shifts the amount by a few cents.

The method in 4 steps

  1. For each game, note the expected match fee: the fixed fee plus round-trip mileage using your rate scale. The full calculation is in our guide to basketball referee match fees.
  2. Link a payer and an expected payment date, based on each body's payment schedule.
  3. Each time a transfer comes in, tick off the games paid. For a grouped transfer, look for the combination of games whose total matches to the cent.
  4. Keep an eye on late payments: a payment past its expected date should be chased quickly, before the trail goes cold.

Real income, not just the match fee

The mileage allowance is a flat rate. What your trip really costs depends on your car and the price of fuel. Referee Tracker works out both, so you know what you really have left after each game and over the whole season. Car-sharing, two games at the same venue, train: the calculation adapts.

At the end of the year

The app adds up your match fees for the season and the calendar year, and tracks the referees' tax-free allowance so you are not caught out when you file your tax return. A yearly export is available on a computer.

Frequently asked questions

How do I know which games a grouped transfer covers?

Add up the match fees of the unpaid games from the same payer until you hit the exact amount. Referee Tracker does it for you: you enter who paid you and how much, and the app finds the combination of games.

Who pays a basketball referee?

It depends on the level and the competition: the county committee, the regional league, the federation or the home club. Each one pays on its own schedule.

What should I do if a referee payment is late?

First check the payer's expected payment date, then chase them with the date and the game number. Referee Tracker alerts you as soon as a payment goes past its expected date.

Does the mileage allowance cover fuel?

Not always exactly: it is a flat rate, while the real cost depends on your car and the price of fuel. The app works out both to give you your real income.
