Guides for basketball referees

Practical guides, with no jargon, to handle everything that happens off the court.

Refereeing a basketball game also means everything that happens before and after it: receiving your appointments, planning your travel, checking your match fees, tracking your payments and improving. These guides answer the questions basketball referees ask themselves, from county level to national championships, in 5x5 and 3x3.

Each guide explains the method to follow by hand, then what a referee tracking app like Referee Tracker can do for you. Start with the one that matches what you need right now.

Why these guides?

They are written by an active referee, based on real situations on court and in end-of-season accounts. They stay deliberately simple: no jargon, concrete examples, lists to follow. Amounts quoted are examples: the official appointment and your committee's, league's or federation's fee scale remain the reference.
